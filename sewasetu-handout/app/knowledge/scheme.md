Scheme eligibility and benefit (portal: About the Scheme and home page)

Residents of Purvanchal who are 60 years of age or older may apply for the Old
Age Pension Scheme. The monthly pension shown on the portal is Rs. 250. One
application per eligible citizen is allowed. The portal's age and application
checks do not constitute an official eligibility decision. A staff member must
review applications and make any official decision.
