Application process and documents (portal application forms)

The application flow includes mobile-number verification by SMS OTP, personal
details, address details, bank details, an age-proof upload, and a declaration.
Accepted age-proof document types listed on the upload page are a birth
certificate, school leaving certificate, or voter ID. Accepted upload formats
are JPG or PDF, up to 5 MB. Applicants should compare any OCR suggestions with
their own document and correct the form themselves. The portal accepts an
applicant's bank account number and IFSC for pension disbursement. Never share
an OTP, password, bank information, or personal documents in the AI assistant.

For help completing the form, contact the nearest Block Development Office.
The assistant cannot submit a form, view an application, or check its status.
