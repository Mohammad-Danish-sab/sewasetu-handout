General staff review checklist (portal application fields and workflow)

Staff should review the submitted application and age-proof document in the
protected staff portal, compare the applicant-entered name and date of birth
with the document, check required form fields and uploaded document presence,
and follow current department policy for any discrepancy. Verify bank details
only through the authorized internal workflow. Keep citizen records private.
This is a general checklist, not a decision recommendation. Only authorized
staff may determine eligibility and approve or reject an application.
